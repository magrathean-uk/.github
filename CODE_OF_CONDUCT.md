# Code of conduct

Magrathean UK repositories and community spaces follow the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Reporting

Report a conduct concern privately to <contact@magrathean.uk>. Include links, dates and the
relevant context. Report a security vulnerability through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, warn,
restrict participation or block an account to protect the project and its participants.
Reports are assessed as fairly and confidentially as practicable.
