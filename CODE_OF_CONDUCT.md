# Code of Conduct

Magrathean UK expects professional, good-faith participation in its public repositories and community spaces.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss the work, evidence, and technical trade-offs rather than attacking people.
- Treat differing experience, background, identity, accessibility needs, and viewpoints with respect.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and accept correction without harassment or retaliation.
- Keep disagreements proportionate and stop when a maintainer closes a line of discussion.
- Protect confidential, personal, security-sensitive, and embargoed information.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest, affiliations, and copied or generated material.

## Unacceptable conduct

- harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, discrimination, or sexualised attention;
- insults, personal attacks, deliberate humiliation, or sustained disruption;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- impersonation, deceptive evidence, spam, coordinated abuse, or malicious reporting;
- pressuring contributors to disclose personal circumstances or private employer/customer information;
- using project channels to facilitate unlawful access, credential theft, malware, evasion, or harm;
- retaliation against a person who raises a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, release channels, and other spaces where a person is representing a Magrathean UK project. Repository-specific community rules may add stricter requirements.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to <contact@magrathean.uk>. Include links, dates, relevant context, and the least amount of personal information needed to assess the report. Security vulnerabilities must use [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, issue warnings, restrict participation, or block accounts where necessary to protect the project and its participants. Reports will be assessed as fairly and confidentially as practicable; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed where investigation, legal duties, or platform enforcement require disclosure.

Bad-faith or knowingly false reports may themselves result in enforcement. Good-faith disagreement, blunt technical criticism, and rejection of a proposal are not misconduct merely because they are unwelcome.
