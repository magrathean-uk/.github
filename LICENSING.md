# Licensing and attribution

## These documents

[LICENSE](LICENSE) contains the controlling repository notice, including the copyright attribution to Magrathean UK Ltd. It reserves all rights and describes why the governance documents, templates, and workflow specifications are published. It does not contain a standard open-source licence grant.

This page explains the existing notice. It does not add permissions, withdraw earlier grants, or change the legal text. For permission or licensing enquiries, use the existing contact address: <contact@magrathean.uk>.

## Projects using these defaults

A project's own licence and any applicable third-party notices govern that project's material. Showing a shared contribution policy or issue form does not replace those terms. GitHub does not inherit a licence from the `.github` repository. See [GitHub's default community-file rules](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/setting-up-your-project-for-healthy-contributions/creating-a-default-community-health-file).

## Contributions and third-party material

External contributions are accepted only for public repositories, subject to their published licence and contribution terms. Private repositories are maintainer-only. This participation rule does not grant a new outbound licence or replace the existing notice.

Follow the affected repository's [contribution policy](CONTRIBUTING.md) and licence. Keep existing copyright and attribution notices with material you propose to reuse. Identify its source and permission terms in the change description. A sign-off records provenance; it does not replace the applicable licence or grant rights the contributor does not hold.
